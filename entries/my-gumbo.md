# My Gumbo

This is the gumbo I make: dark roux, Conecuh sausage, rotisserie chicken, shrimp, and a lot of patience.

## Ingredients

- Conecuh sausage
- About 1 cup vegetable oil
- About 1 cup flour
- Minced onion
- Minced garlic
- Green bell pepper
- About 5 cups chicken broth, plus more as needed
- Creole seasoning
- A little liquid seafood boil
- Shredded rotisserie chicken
- Shrimp
- Cooked rice, for serving

## How I Make It

1. Chop the Conecuh sausage and cook it over high heat until the pieces are crispy and leave a coating on the bottom of the pot.
2. Make the roux with about 1 cup vegetable oil and 1 cup flour. Stir it for around 50 minutes, until it becomes a deep dark brown.
3. Add the minced onion, garlic, and green bell pepper. Let them cook in the roux for a while as you keep stirring.
4. Slowly add about 5 cups of chicken broth while stirring. Add more broth if the gumbo needs it.
5. Season with Creole seasoning and a little liquid seafood boil. Let it simmer for a while so the flavors come together.
6. Add the shredded rotisserie chicken and the cooked sausage.
7. Once everything is hot, add the shrimp during the last few minutes and cook just until the shrimp are done.
8. Turn the heat to low and let everything simmer together for a while.
9. Serve over rice.

The part that takes the longest is the roux, but that dark roux is what makes the whole thing taste like gumbo instead of just soup.
